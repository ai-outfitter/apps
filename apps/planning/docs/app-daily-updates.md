# Daily Wiki updates

Workshop, Software Foundry and Onto each offer a daily schedule at 02:00 in the installation’s reporting timezone. Enable the schedule in each participating installation. Workshop reports project revisions, Foundry reports merged pull requests from the organization’s connected repositories, and Onto reports updated change batches. Personal installations of Workshop and Onto write to the user’s Wiki. Organization installations write to the organization’s Wiki.

Planning writes its app update when its owner’s daily digest runs. Its organization overview reads the four app documents for the same local date and timezone and records their revisions. A missing or late document leaves the overview incomplete. Rerun Planning after late updates arrive to refresh it.

Forerunner’s personal morning brief reads the Wiki’s federated DailyAppUpdates collection for yesterday’s calendar date across the member’s personal Wiki and accessible organization Wikis, including organizations without Planning. Each source names its own timezone and links to the saved revision. A rerun of today’s brief picks up late or revised updates. App updates use the Wiki’s generated draft section and preserve authored notes and existing publication history.
