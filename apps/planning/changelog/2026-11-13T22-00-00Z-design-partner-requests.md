Design partners can file feature and bug requests with their provider's Planning from their own organization. A design partner is a customer whose `customer_of` link to the provider has the new `design_partner` flag (Onto's standard types now carry it). Editors refresh the partner list from the graph (`RefreshDesignPartners`), review requests (`PartnerRequests`), and accept one, which files it with Software Foundry's `RequestChanges` with the partner named, or reject it with a reason the partner reads back by replaying its request. `SubmitPartnerRequest` is the only Planning field open to other organizations. See `docs/design-partner-requests.md`.
