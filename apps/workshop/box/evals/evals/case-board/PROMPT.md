# Mini-ITX board envelope

A Mini-ITX board as a fit envelope: a 170 x 170 x 1.6 mm plate with four 3.2 mm holes, 33.02 and 165.1 mm from the left edge and 10.16 and 165.1 mm in from the rear edge. On its top face a block for the rear connectors, 154.75 mm wide and 40 mm tall, starting 7.625 mm from the left edge, running from 3 mm inside the rear edge to 4 mm beyond it.
