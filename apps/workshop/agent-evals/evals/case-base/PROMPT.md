# Case base with standoff bosses

A 224 x 178 x 3 mm plate. Four 7 mm diameter bosses 2.5 mm tall on top, centred 37.02 and 169.1 mm from the left edge and 8.9 and 163.84 mm from the front edge, each with a 3.3 mm hole through boss and plate. Two 3 mm wide, 6 mm tall stops on top: a 150 mm rail starting 173.7 mm from the left and 21.5 mm from the front, running back, and a 43.3 mm bar starting at the same left position and 18.2 mm from the front, running right.
