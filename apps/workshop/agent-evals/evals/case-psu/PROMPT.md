# Flex ATX power supply envelope

A Flex ATX power supply as a fit envelope, standing on its long edge: a 40 mm wide, 150 mm deep, 81.5 mm tall block. On its rear face (the +Y end, toward the back of the case) an IEC inlet block 27 mm wide, 19.5 mm tall and 5 mm proud, 3.4 mm from its -X side and 5 mm below its top.
