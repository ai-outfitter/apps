# L bracket

An L bracket 30 mm wide: a base 50 mm long and 5 mm thick, and an upright 5 mm thick and 40 mm tall (overall) at one end of the base. A 6 mm hole through the base, centred across its width and 30 mm from the upright's outer face, and a 6 mm hole through the upright, centred across its width and 25 mm above the bottom of the base.
