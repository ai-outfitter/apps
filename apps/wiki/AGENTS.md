# Wiki documents and pages

- Wiki owns documents, pages, immutable revisions, generation and publication.
- Onto owns entities, relationships and graph history. Read it through scoped host contracts; references never grant access.
- Preserve existing `outfitter/wiki-map` identity, mounts and migrations. Add only forward migrations. Source graph tables are historical archives.
- Authored content and generated sections are separate. Generation defaults to drafts and never silently replaces published content.
- Governance owns RAPID duties and authority. Required policy decisions bind to exact revision/digest; unavailable or stale policy blocks publication.
- App-owned runtime, UI, workflows, tests and migrations belong here. Use public SDKs only; fixtures must be synthetic.
