# Use platform automation and connection management

Remove Forerunner's settings, source-management and workflow-management screens and their client scripts. Old URLs navigate to the platform Automations or Connections pages with an ordinary link fallback. The sidebar no longer lists duplicate Sources and Workflows; settings opens platform schedules. App-owned jobs and GraphQL contracts remain supported.

Discovery declares its required account and input mappings in workflow metadata. The platform must deploy support for this declaration before this app package is published. Organization links retain their workspace mount.
