# Deliver the daily brief before the user wakes

Daily brief saves now explicitly schedule 05:00 in a required, validated user-selected timezone. Settings detects the device timezone and offers an explicit update when it differs from the saved timezone. Planning's defaults move to 03:00 for organization reports and 04:00 for personal reports; existing schedules require resaving. The brief still reads yesterday's complete local day and reports unavailable sources rather than assuming time-based ordering guarantees readiness.
