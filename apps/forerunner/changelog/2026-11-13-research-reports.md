# Save a daily research report to the Wiki

Forerunner gains research reports: a member saves up to three reports, each with a title, who it is for, a place and up to six topics (for example local artist events, open calls, residencies and volunteer opportunities), plus an hour and timezone. Each morning `forerunner-research-report` searches the public web per topic through the platform's `web.search`, asks inference to pick what is relevant and still open, keeps only items that cite a page a search returned, and saves a dated, sourced document per report to the personal Wiki. `ResearchReportSchedule`, `SetResearchReportSchedule` and `GenerateResearchReports` are published to MCP clients. It needs a platform that serves `web.search`; until then each report records that search was unavailable and saves nothing.
