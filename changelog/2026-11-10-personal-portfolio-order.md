# Personal portfolio ordering

Allow personal Planning installations to order their own projects/products and
publish independent organization and federated project ordering documents.
Resolve allowed records through the member's live authorization, keep complete
list/revision checks, omit revoked references, and bound pagination. Shared
organization orders remain unchanged.

Validation covers owner isolation, authorization, stale revisions, duplicates,
incomplete lists, federation pagination and provider failure. This change does
not activate schedules or change any live portfolio order.
