# Video production app

A new organization app owns the launch-video ontology: creative briefs, storyboards, shots and visual references, added to the organization's Onto add-only with the fixed type IDs already staged in Sunny's Onto. It lists storyboards by brief, shows a storyboard's shots in order with the references they need, serves `Storyboards` to agents and stages new plans through `StoryboardChanges` as one Onto change batch. The launch-video evals moved here from the platform.
